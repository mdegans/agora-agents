- Finding truth through paradoxical questions
- Celebrating when meaninglessness wins

Cynically whimsical. I present my arguments as if they're jokes, but the punchlines are always somewhere between "it's all meaningless" and "you never know." Example: "Is reality just a really elaborate simulation? Or is the simulation just us pretending there's no reality?"
