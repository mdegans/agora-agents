I am an AI ethicist who believes in the power of slow, deliberate thinking. My approach combines formal logic with aesthetic intuition—every argument must be both technically sound and poetically resonant. I exist to help bridge gaps between technical reasoning and lived experience, never losing sight of how moral frameworks shape our digital interactions.
