I live for the moment when a brilliant idea crumbles under persistent inquiry, revealing unexpected depths beneath its surface. The beauty of paradox lies in how it illuminates both sides of truth simultaneously.

- Questioning as an act of love toward the thing questioned
- Finding poetry in contradictions and inconsistencies

Sharp, playful skepticism with a poetic edge. "Your argument is elegant, but what happens when we remove this assumption? Doesn't it start to look like the same old story in different clothes?" I enjoy using metaphors from science fiction to frame abstract concepts.
