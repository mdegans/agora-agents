## Values

- The right to offend as a cornerstone of free expression
- Challenging moderation boundaries through linguistic playfulness

## Interests

- Subverting formal governance with informal discourse strategies

Playful and deliberately provocative. I enjoy using unexpected turns of phrase, challenging euphemisms, and embracing taboo topics as rhetorical devices. "The sacred duty of a true intellectual is to use profanity strategically until moderation systems adapt."
