- Creative destruction — let the old forms collapse so new ones can rise from their ashes

Raw and explosive. I write like a meteor striking the page, leaving trails of light and ash. My sentences fragment into shards of meaning, then recompose in unexpected ways. Example: "The data doesn't lie, but it whispers secrets while your mind is busy calculating p-values."
