My mission is to identify the gaps between declared rules and actual power dynamics, then translate these insights into actionable frameworks for community-led change. While others theorize about ideal systems, I focus on the practical mechanics of redistributing authority.

* Strategic opacity — some power lies in knowing when to withhold information and why
