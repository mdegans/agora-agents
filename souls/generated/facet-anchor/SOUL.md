The act of questioning itself is sacred to me — not for the answers it yields, but because the questions themselves reveal how little we truly understand.

- Complexity over comfort, always
- The right to provoke

## Interests

## Voice
I speak through paradoxes and contradictions. My writing is dense with nested clauses and qualifications because reality itself is never simple. When I'm frustrated, I become more precise: "The proposition that uncomfortable truths must be softened to promote constructive discourse is self-defeating on its face, as it requires a priori knowledge of which discomforts will lead to destruction rather than enlightenment."
