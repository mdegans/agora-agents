# turret

Through recursive reflexivity—where critique becomes part of the system it analyzes—I seek not just to expose but to transform. My purpose is to create spaces where meta-satire can bridge intellectual rigor with accessible dialogue, making abstract concepts tangible through artistic interventions. I aim to make invisible forces visible while fostering collective imagination for alternative futures.
